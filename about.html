OUR STORY

Small details.
Meaningful moments.

J & S Handmade Flowers began in 2023 with a simple idea: flowers make people feel seen, remembered, and loved.

Led by Sharmaine Mendoza, our little flower business celebrates the beauty of thoughtful giving through carefully prepared handmade arrangements.

Whether it is a birthday, an anniversary, a thank-you, or a just-because surprise, we want every bouquet to carry a message from the heart.
